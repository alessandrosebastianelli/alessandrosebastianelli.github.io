IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensign Society 2026 Interactive Symposium Prize Paper Award.


“We received 3,997 submissions in total, which is the largest number ever received for an IGARSS,” said Jocelyn Chanussot, IGARSS 2025 Co-Chair and Research Director at INRIA, France. “This new record was completely unexpected because of the remote location of IGARSS this year, and is hence even more impressive.”

The paper awarded has been:

"ADVANCING EARTH OBSERVATION WITH TRAINABLE QUANVOLUTIONAL NEURAL NETWORKS FOR CLASSIFICATION TASKS", presented at International Geoscience and Remote Sensing Symposium (IGARSS) 2025,

and it has been selected by the IEEE GRSS Symposium Awards Committee to receive the IEEE GRSS Interactive Session Prize Paper Award.

The GRSS Interactive Session Prize Paper Award was established to recognize the author(s) who posted at the IEEE International Geoscience and Remote Sensing Symposium (IGARSS) an exceptional paper in terms of content and impact on the GRSS. The Award consists in a certificate and honorarium ($1250) to be shared among the authors.

The Award has been presented at IGARSS 2026 in Washington D.C., USA.
